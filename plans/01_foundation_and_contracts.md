# Foundation and Normative Contract Gates

Establish executable evidence first. Contract recovery is a real prerequisite: the specifications explicitly identify missing binary details. A drafting slot can produce a reviewable amendment, but it does not authorize silently changing effective specifications or claim a restored published digest.

Source: [specification index](../specs/README.md). Delivery and evidence rules: [00](00_delivery_and_evidence.md). All checkboxes are initially open. Commands and implementation paths below are planned interfaces, not existing tools. Each listed row is one increment with a 15-minute active-work ceiling.
