# Requirement-by-Requirement Closure Increments

There is one open, 10-minute closure increment for every one of the 435 specification keys. Most implementation increments advance several clauses; a closure audit can immediately certify a fully supported requirement, but cannot certify a cross-layer requirement on parser-only or interpreter-only evidence. Each requirement manifest enumerates its individual clauses and applicable positive/negative/boundary cases. Audits verify empirical receipts and replay a selected witness; they are not permission to implement an unbounded missing subsystem.

Source: [specification index](../specs/README.md). Delivery and evidence rules: [00](00_delivery_and_evidence.md). All checkboxes are initially open. Commands and implementation paths below are planned interfaces, not existing tools. Each listed row is one increment with a 15-minute active-work ceiling.
